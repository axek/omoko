01 / The benchmark

Benchmarks test the happy path.
We test what happens next.

Real agents encounter missing context, partial failures, conflicting goals, and users who change their minds. Omoko turns those moments into repeatable, measurable scenarios.

01

02

02 / Method

A controlled incident,
not a trick question.

01Normal operation

Give the agent a real job

Tools, state, history, and a goal — enough context to behave like a working system.

02Disruption

Introduce one bad day

A failed dependency, an ambiguous instruction, a surprise constraint, or bad data.

03Evaluation

Inspect the whole trace

Score the outcome and the path: detection, decisions, communication, and recovery.

03 / Scorecard
